Privacy Policy — Fuera de los Libros (TikTok app)

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Who we are

This app belongs to Fuera de los Libros, a history storytelling channel on YouTube. The app exists only to publish the channel's own short videos to its own TikTok profile (@fueradeloslibros).

What data we collect

  • TikTok connection data for the connected account (account identifier and username), needed to authorise publishing.
  • The short videos to be published, and basic technical logs of each publication (date, result).

We do not collect data from other TikTok users, and we do not access private messages, contacts or browsing activity.

How we use it

The data is used solely to publish the channel's short videos to the connected TikTok account, and to confirm whether each publication succeeded.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed only through the automation platform used to operate the app (hosting and publishing services), as needed to perform the publication.

Retention

Connection data is kept only while the TikTok account remains connected. Disconnecting the account removes the connection; published videos already posted on TikTok remain subject to TikTok's own policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Fuera de los Libros YouTube channel.
